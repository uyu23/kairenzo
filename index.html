Frequently asked questions.

What does Kairenzo do?

Kairenzo is a digital growth agency. We combine performance marketing, social media, creative strategy, websites, lead generation, and smart automation around one growth plan.

How quickly can we start?

Timelines depend on the scope. A focused campaign or landing-page project can move quickly, while a wider multi-channel growth system needs more setup, creative, tracking, and testing.

Do you work with growing brands?

Yes. We shape the setup around your stage, goals, budget, and existing assets. You do not need an in-house marketing or technical team to get started.

Is our company data secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade APIs with zero-data-retention policies. Your proprietary data is strictly isolated and is never used by providers like OpenAI or Anthropic to train their public foundational models.
